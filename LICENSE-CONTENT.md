## Why ShareAlike

A standard is worth what its adoption is worth, so the licence permits nearly
everything: citation, commercial use, embedding inside larger documents,
teaching. The one thing worth preventing is a fork that closes — a vendor
altering the clauses to match what their product happens to do, then publishing
the result as a proprietary standard. ShareAlike makes that impossible.

The cost is real: third-party translations and adaptations inherit the licence,
which some organisations will not accept. That trade is deliberate.
